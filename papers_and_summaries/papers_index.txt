LIPIcs.SNAPL.2017.8.pdf - Dill
 - this paper goes into the current difficulties of intermittent computing operation, namely:
 - - control flow after shutdown and start (where does the program restart from?)
 - - memory inconsistency (data rolled back to a checkpoint after a restart, so data is lost)
 - - environmental sensor concurrency (sensors usually operated on interrupts, which can be affected by shutdowns)
 - - energy storage and distribution

A Low-Power Dual-Factor Authentication Unit.pdf - Audrey: 
-   Designs a hardware-accelerated security handshake to prevent 
    remote energy-drain attacks, but leaves open how to securely 
    authenticate a system that is designed to constantly die and 
    reboot mid-operation.

Ramadass-2010-A batteryless thermoelectric energy-harvesting interface 
circuit with 35mV startup voltage.pdf - Audrey: 
-   Demonstrates how to harvest a microscopic 35 mV signal to slowly 
    trickle-charge a capacitor, but leaves open the digital logic 
    required to safely execute math on that fragile, easily drained 
    energy puddle.

Intermittent-Aware Design Exploration of Systolic Array Using.pdf - Audrey: 
-   Simulates the theory of saving hardware accelerator data to Non-Volatile 
    Memory (NVM) during brownouts, but leaves open the physical RTL 
    implementation and strict timing proof that your FPGA project will 
    actually build.

Improving engineered biological systems.pdf - Audrey 
-   Establishes that sensors need long-term microfluidic isolation to 
    capture slow biological events, but leaves open how the hardware will 
    stitch that data together across dozens of inevitable power deaths.

Integrated Real-Time CMOS Luminescence Sensing and.pdf - Audrey
-   Integrates CMOS electronics with microfluidics for real-time biological 
    sensing, but leaves open how to sustain this continuous data 
    collection when the ambient power source unpredictably fails.
